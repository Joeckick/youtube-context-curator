## What I'm working on

- Building the case for a proper experimentation platform

## Career goals

- **Short-term:** Get promoted to Lead PM by end of year
- **Longer-term:** Become a product leader at a company I believe in, ideally with equity upside

## What I care about learning

- How to develop better product intuition
- Pricing strategy for consumer products
- Building and leading small product teams
- Using AI tools to ship faster as a PM
- Discovery techniques that actually work

## Core beliefs

- Great PMs are close to customers, not just shipping features
- Product sense can be developed but requires deliberate practice
- The best insights come from watching users, not reading reports

## What's NOT useful for me

- Fundraising content (not my domain)
